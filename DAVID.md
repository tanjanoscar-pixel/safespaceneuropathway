## The Six Principles of Lived Experience Design

### 1. Meet People Where They Are

When someone opens this app, they may be in crisis, or they may be curious, or they may be doing well and wanting to maintain that. We do not assume.

- No onboarding that requires vulnerability before trust is established.
- No "how are you feeling today?" before the user has a reason to trust us.
- The archetype quiz is not therapy — it is a mirror. It asks who you are, not what is wrong with you.
- No deficit framing. We describe patterns of coping, not disorders.

### 2. Language Matters Enormously

Words used in mental health spaces carry weight that researchers in other fields do not always appreciate. Our language rules:

**Never use:**
- "Crazy", "insane", "psycho" (even casually)
- "Triggered" as a joke or diminutive
- "Just" (as in "just breathe", "just try this") — minimises real difficulty
- "You should…" — directive language creates shame when people cannot comply
- "Positive thinking" framing — toxic positivity causes real harm
- "Everything happens for a reason"
- "Strong people don't ask for help" (or its inverse as inspiration)

**Instead use:**
- "I see you" — acknowledgement without judgement
- "You're safe here" — safety before anything else
- "This is hard" — validation of the real difficulty
- "When you're ready" — autonomy, no pressure
- "Some people find…" — offering without prescribing
- "You don't have to explain yourself" — permission to just exist

OneNoir's greetings are written to this standard. Every string that displays to a user must pass this test: *would this sentence be said by someone who has actually been through this?*

### 3. No Toxic Positivity

The affirmations in Phase 2 are "David-approved." This means:

- They do not deny pain. "You are strong" is less honest than "You have survived things that were hard."
- They do not demand performance. "Choose happiness" is not recovery — it is theatre.
- They acknowledge complexity. "Healing is not linear" is real. "Every day gets better" often isn't.
- They give permission to rest. The system has rewarded relentless pushing. We do not.

The test: *would someone who is having a genuinely bad day feel worse after reading this?* If yes, it does not ship.

### 4. The Archetype System Is Not Diagnosis

The six archetypes (Warrior, Sage, Empath, Creator, Healer, Anchor) describe *coping styles and ways of moving through the world* — not disorders, not fixed identities, not diagnostic categories.

A person can be a Warrior who has elements of Anchor. A Healer can have Sage tendencies. The archetype is a starting point for self-understanding, not a box.

- Never frame an archetype as better or worse than another.
- All six have genuine strengths and genuine challenges.
- The description of each archetype must feel like recognition, not assignment.
- No archetype implies pathology.

If we add the ability to re-take the quiz (Phase 2), we frame it as growth, not correction.

### 5. Trust Is Built Slowly

People who have been through trauma have often had trust violated by systems that claimed to help. The first interaction with ALTerEgo must feel safe before it asks anything.

- The landing page does not ask for anything. It offers.
- Registration is low-friction — only email and password required; display name is optional.
- The quiz is framed as discovery, not assessment.
- OneNoir speaks before the user does — it sets the tone.
- We do not ask for phone number, address, or any identifying information beyond email in Phase 1.
- Trusted contacts (Phase 3) are entirely user-controlled. We do not suggest contacting them.

### 6. The User Owns Their Data

This is both a GDPR requirement and a principle of respect.

- Users can see everything we store about them.
- Users can delete their account and all associated data.
- We never share data with third parties (see ALTEREGO.md).
- We never use data to make clinical assessments.
- We never send communications that exploit emotional vulnerabilities (no "we noticed you haven't logged in..." messages).

## OneNoir — The Guide

OneNoir is not a chatbot. OneNoir is not an AI therapist. OneNoir is a presence — a visual companion that acknowledges you are here.

The name: "One Noir" — the singular dark, the one who has also been in the dark.

OneNoir's greetings are written from a place of having been there. They are not aspirational. They are *recognitional*.

OneNoir never:
- Gives advice
- Asks probing questions
- Suggests the user should feel differently
- Uses clinical language
- Claims to understand what the user is going through specifically

OneNoir always:
- Acknowledges presence ("I see you")
- Offers safety ("You're safe here")
- Meets the archetype where it is (archetype-specific greetings)
- Speaks in first person, as a companion not an authority
